How it works

Install it and keep browsing

Shopee Likes uses your existing Shopee login. There is no separate account to create.

  1. 1

    Add the extension

    Install it from the Chrome Web Store when the listing is available. Until then, you can load it manually from GitHub.

  2. 2

    Browse Shopee as usual

    Open your local Shopee site and make sure you’re logged in. Then continue browsing as usual.

  3. 3

    Open “My favorites”

    Use the floating heart button or the toolbar icon to open a panel with the products you’ve liked.

Features

What Shopee Likes does

The extension focuses on showing your favorites and asks for only the access it needs.

No tracking or storage

The extension has no analytics, trackers, or backend server. It does not save your account data.

View only

It can’t like, unlike, or buy anything. It only displays the favorites already saved to your Shopee account.

Eight markets

Works with Shopee sites in Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, and Vietnam.

Prices in your local format

Prices use the currency and number format for your Shopee market, such as R$ in Brazil or NT$ in Taiwan.

Access limited to Shopee

The extension runs only on the eight supported Shopee sites. It does not read your other tabs or ask for your password.

Simple, open-source code

The extension is plain JavaScript with no runtime dependencies or build step. You can read the source on GitHub.

Privacy & security

Your favorites stay in your browser

Shopee Likes uses the Shopee login already active in your browser and loads your favorites directly from Shopee. It does not have a server of its own.

It runs only on supported Shopee sites. It can display your favorites, but it cannot change your account or send your data to us.

  • No analytics. The extension does not include ads or tracking.

  • No password access. It uses the Shopee login already active in your browser without reading your password.

  • Nothing stored. Your favorites are loaded when you open the panel and are not saved by the extension.

  • Only Shopee sites. Chrome permissions are limited to the eight supported Shopee domains.

  • Source available. The code is MIT licensed and available on GitHub.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Shopee Likes affiliated with Shopee?

No. Shopee Likes is an independent, open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shopee or Sea Group.

Does it collect or send my data anywhere?

No. The extension has no analytics, trackers, or backend server. It loads favorites and product images directly from Shopee and does not send them anywhere else.

Why do I need to be logged in to Shopee?

Shopee needs to know which favorites belong to you. The extension uses the Shopee login already active in your browser, but it does not read your password. If you log out, the panel cannot load the list.

Can it like or unlike products for me?

No. It can only display your saved favorites. Clicking a product opens its Shopee page in a new tab.

What if the panel stops loading one day?

Shopee does not publish or guarantee the favorites interface used by the extension, so a Shopee update may temporarily break it. An expired login, CAPTCHA, or content blocker can also stop the panel from loading. When that happens, updates will be published on GitHub.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, has no ads or paid tier, and is MIT licensed.

Want your favorites back on desktop?

Shopee Likes puts your saved products in a panel you can open from any supported Shopee page. It is free and open source.
